How it works

Three taps between you and the real price.

1

Search or scan

Type a medicine name, or photograph the strip, box or prescription.

2

See every price

DawaCheck finds the active salt and lists the same salt at every price, cheapest first.

Decide, with your doctor

Take the comparison to your doctor or pharmacist, or find the nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendra.
