Delete your DawaCheck account

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in DawaCheck (Android app, developer: DawaCheck, contact support@dawacheck.in) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open DawaCheck and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap "Delete my account" and confirm.

Your account is deleted from our server immediately. If you have a monthly plan, its renewal is stopped; if that ever fails, the app tells you to cancel it in Google Play.

Option 2: without the app

Email support@dawacheck.in from the email address of the Google account you signed in with (or, for older accounts, include the phone number you verified), with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Google account details (account ID, name, email address, profile photo) or phone number
  • Your check count, subscription and recharge records
  • Your saved My List and savings history

What is kept

  • For 35 days: a one-way coded identifier with today's check count, this month's subscription usage and whether you answered the age/location questions, so the free allowance can't be reset by re-creating the account. It is then erased automatically.
  • Anonymous records of age range, gender, approximate location and searched medicines. These are stored without any name, email address, phone number or account identifier, are not joined to your account, and are not removed.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of any purchases under Google's policies.
